## 👋 About Us

We are **BreakPoint**, a student-driven FRC team founded in **2026** in **Bursa, Türkiye**. Our members come from different schools and meet at **Açık Atölye Tech Hub**, where we design, build and program competition robots together.

We believe robotics is about more than building a robot. It is about learning to think like engineers, working as a team, and solving real problems.

## 🤖 Our Work

- **Robot Code:** command-based Java robot software built on WPILib, with full physics simulation and PathPlanner autonomous paths
- **[FRC Management](https://github.com/BreakPoint-FRC/FRC-Management):** our own team management app (roles, meeting attendance, task tracking with Gantt view, and a sponsorship tracker), built with Next.js, Fastify and PostgreSQL
